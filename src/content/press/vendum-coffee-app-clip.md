Plumstead roastery VENDUM Coffee has launched an iPhone app that lets customers reorder by scanning the bag. There is a code on the back of every bag. Scan it when the coffee runs out, confirm with Apple Pay, and the next bag is on its way.

The scan opens an App Clip, a small part of the VENDUM Coffee app that loads instantly and disappears afterwards. Customers do not need to install anything, create an account or remember a password. The same code works at the VENDUM Coffee market stall, for anyone who would rather tap than queue.

The full app, free on the App Store, adds the rest of the range and lets subscribers manage their deliveries.

> "The App Clip catches someone at the exact moment they notice the bag is empty, before that thought turns into a note on a shopping list nobody ever looks at again."
>
> Philip Brumpton, founder of VENDUM Coffee

The app was designed and built by Brumpton, who founded the roastery and is a product designer by trade. Payments run through Stripe, and every order passes straight into the shop's order system without anyone copying it across.

> "It's the most technically interesting thing I've built for the business. It doesn't win a single new customer, though. It makes life easier for the people the markets and the leaflets already brought in."
>
> Philip Brumpton

The VENDUM Coffee app is free on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id6748237165), with more at [vendum.coffee](https://vendum.coffee).
