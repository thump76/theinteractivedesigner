Plumstead has its own coffee roastery. VENDUM Coffee roasts small batches to order in SE18 and sells them online, through its own iPhone app, and from a stall at local markets in Woolwich and Plumstead.

VENDUM Coffee was founded by Philip Brumpton, a product designer who has lived in South East London since 1999. He roasts, packs and delivers the coffee himself, and designed and built the shop, the app and the systems behind them.

The range is deliberately short. Coffee Morning is an everyday blend of Colombian and Brazilian beans. Common Espresso is its darker sibling, for espresso machines and moka pots. Radical Coffee Club changes origin every month, for people who want to try something different each time the bag runs out.

> "Plumstead's premier coffee roaster. Also its only one. Everything is roasted to order, so the beans in your bag were green a few days ago, not sitting in a warehouse for months."
>
> Philip Brumpton, founder of VENDUM Coffee

Subscriptions run on a system Brumpton built himself. When the fulfilment platform he started on removed a feature he relied on and offered to sell it back at more than double the price, he replaced it with Stripe for payments and Make.com to pass each order into the shop automatically. It costs a fraction of the platform it replaced, and the customer data stays with the business.

> "It's the most useful thing I've built for the business, and it taught me the lesson I keep relearning. The things that actually bring people in are standing behind a table at a market and putting leaflets through letterboxes."
>
> Philip Brumpton

Delivery is free across the SE18 and SE2 postcode districts. VENDUM Coffee also supplies cafes and local businesses wholesale.
