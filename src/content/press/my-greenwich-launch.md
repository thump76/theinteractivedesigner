A free iPhone app that tells residents of the Royal Borough of Greenwich who represents them is now available on the App Store. My Greenwich was designed and built by Philip Brumpton, a product designer who lives in Plumstead.

Enter a Greenwich postcode and the app shows which of the borough's 23 wards it is in, the councillors for that ward with their party and how to contact them, and the MP for the constituency. Greenwich has 55 councillors, two or three for each ward. Everything links back to the council's and Parliament's own pages.

> "Most people I talk to locally couldn't tell you which ward they live in, let alone who their councillors are. The information is all public, it's just spread across several websites. I wanted one screen that answers the question and then gets out of the way."
>
> Philip Brumpton, designer of My Greenwich

The app is neutral by design. Councillors are shown in the same way whichever party they represent, with no commentary and no ratings. There is no sign-up, no advertising and no tracking, and the postcode a resident enters stays on their phone.

My Greenwich also includes a directory of independent businesses, sole traders and community groups across the borough, sorted by category and shown nearest first. A basic listing is free and every submission is checked by hand before it appears. Chains and franchises are not listed.

> "The directory is free to join and sorted by distance, so the cafe or trader nearest to you comes first. It's a small way of putting independent businesses in front of the people who live round the corner from them."
>
> Philip Brumpton

The app covers all 23 wards, from Thamesmead Moorings to Eltham Page. It was built to the accessibility standards expected of local government services, with support for larger text, VoiceOver and reduced motion.

My Greenwich is the third civic app from Brumpton, after [Who Is My MP?](/press/who-is-my-mp-launch/) and [The Lords](/press/the-lords-launch/), which use Parliament's public data. He plans to bring the same approach to other London boroughs, and is open to working with councils that want to manage their own content.

My Greenwich is free on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/my-greenwich/id6803486290), with more at [mygreenwich.uk](https://mygreenwich.uk).
