Bikes&.co, a new cycling brand from Plumstead in South East London, has opened its online shop and launched an iPhone app, with a deliberately short range of gear for people who ride to work.

The range is three things: cycling socks, a coffee flask and a musette, the small cotton bag riders carry their food in. Each is chosen for the commute rather than the race.

> "Socks, a flask and a musette. That's the range. I'd rather make three things properly than fill a catalogue."
>
> Philip Brumpton, founder of Bikes&.co

The shop and the app were designed and built by Brumpton, a product designer who also runs Plumstead roastery VENDUM Coffee. Orders in the app are paid securely through Stripe and passed to the shop automatically.

Bikes&.co has also launched a [GPS theft tracker](/press/bikesand-co-gps-tracker/) for bikes, with its own companion app.

More at [bikesand.co](https://bikesand.co). The Bikes&.co app is free on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id6787139917).
