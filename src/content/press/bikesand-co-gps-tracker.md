Bikes&.co, a cycling brand from Plumstead in South East London, has launched a GPS theft tracker for bikes, with a companion iPhone app.

The tracker is small enough to hide on the bike. Once it is fitted, the free Bikes&.co app shows where the bike is on a map. If the bike moves when the owner hasn't moved it, the app sends an alert straight away, giving the best chance of finding it before it disappears.

Each tracker is set up and tested before it is posted, with its own data SIM already active, so there is nothing to configure beyond pairing it with the app.

> "Bike theft in London is depressingly normal, and most stolen bikes are never seen again. A lock slows a thief down. A tracker you can't see tells you where the bike went."
>
> Philip Brumpton, founder of Bikes&.co

The tracker, the app and the system behind them were designed and built by Brumpton, a product designer who also runs Plumstead roastery VENDUM Coffee.

More at [bikesand.co](https://bikesand.co). The companion app is free on the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/id6787140815).
