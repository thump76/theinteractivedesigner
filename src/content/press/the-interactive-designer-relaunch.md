Product designer Philip Brumpton has relaunched theinteractivedesigner.co.uk, a domain he has owned for twenty years, as a fast, database-free home for his writing and everything he makes.

The site is built with Astro and published as plain HTML. Every post, app listing and press release is a flat file in a GitHub repository, edited through Sveltia CMS, which saves straight to the repository with no server in between. It is hosted on Cloudflare, and the only running cost is the domain.

> "Who's JSON, and why is he now controlling my website? I wanted a site that would still be standing in twenty years without me patching a database. Flat files, plain HTML, nothing to log in to and nothing to break."
>
> Philip Brumpton

The design is deliberately plain: Helvetica, black type on white, and a single red, yellow and blue band under the masthead. There are no webfonts, no analytics and no cookie banner.

The site now acts as the hub for Brumpton's apps and ventures, linking out to Who Is My MP?, The Lords, My Greenwich, KeyCut, VENDUM Coffee, Bikes&.co, P100 and The Negative Scanner. Selected posts are published to LinkedIn automatically, through an RSS feed watched by Make.com.

> "I've designed websites since 1999 and I still write the CSS. This is the first one of my own I've been completely happy with."
>
> Philip Brumpton

Visit [theinteractivedesigner.co.uk](https://theinteractivedesigner.co.uk).
